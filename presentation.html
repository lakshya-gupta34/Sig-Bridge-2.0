Market Analysis

Why Existing AI Sign Tools Fail in India

❌ 1. The "ASL Fallacy"

95% of AI models use American Sign Language. ASL is 1-handed, but Indian Sign Language (ISL) uses 2-handed fingerspelling. They are completely mutually unintelligible!

❌ 2. Grammar Ignorance

Existing tools swap words one-to-one in English SVO order. In ISL, this produces broken "Signed English" that native signers find confusing.

❌ 3. Zero Hindi Support

Over 60% of India communicates in Hindi, but existing research only produces English text, excluding rural healthcare and public desks.

Linguistic Engineering

Authentic ISL SOV Grammar Modeling

Spoken English (SVO)
"Where is the hospital?"
Spoken Hindi (SOV)
"अस्पताल कहाँ है?"
ISL Gloss Sequence (Time-SOV)
[ HOSPITAL ] [ WHERE ]
• Time-First Anchor: Temporal markers (Today, Tomorrow, Now) anchor the sentence head.
• Copula Elimination: Auxiliary verbs (is, am, are, was, है, था) are stripped per ISLRTC rules.
• WH-Question Movement: Question particles migrate to terminus with non-manual eyebrow inflection.
